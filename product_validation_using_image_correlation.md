# Product Validation Using Image Correlation - Prototype/Mockup

## Overview of the Mockup

We are building a prototype/demo of an image correlation model, that looks at two things - the display-picture of a product on the e-commerce website, and a clicked photo of the same taken by the product owner. The result of this would be a correlation/similarity score between the two photos. This would essentially categorize it also, into Unclear Photo, Incorrect Product (bad score), Pass, Flag (used exactly same photo, not a camera photo).
